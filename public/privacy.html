Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TradeGuru handles information when you visit the website, sign in, submit a chart for analysis, or request paid access. It describes the current Service behavior.

1. Information we process

  • Account and session: When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, email address, and available display name to authenticate you and maintain your account. A signed, HTTP-only session cookie keeps you signed in for up to seven days or until you sign out.
  • Chart and analysis: When you request analysis, the chart image you selected, the selected instrument label, and your trade-bias preference are sent to our server and Google Gemini for processing. The resulting analysis is returned to your browser.
  • Payment and access: If you request paid access, we process your account identifier, email, display name, payment-request identifier, submitted UPI transaction reference, review status, and related timestamps. Authorized payment reviewers can access information needed to verify and review a submitted payment.
  • Technical information: Our hosting and service providers may process request and device information, such as IP address, browser details, and service logs, to deliver, maintain, and secure the website and APIs.

2. How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, provide the chart-analysis service you request, create and verify one-month access, prevent duplicate transaction-reference submissions, administer payment reviews, protect the Service, and respond to support requests.

3. When information is shared

We share information with service providers only as needed to operate the Service: Google provides identity verification and Gemini AI processing; Supabase stores account access and manual-payment review records; and our hosting provider delivers the website and server APIs. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

Payment-review information may be accessed by authorized TradeGuru reviewers to verify a UPI transaction. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, security, and integrity of users or the Service. We do not sell personal information.

4. Chart images and payment details

Chart images are sent for analysis only when you request an analysis. TradeGuru does not store uploaded chart images in the subscription database. Google Gemini processes the image to generate the requested result and may handle it under Google's applicable service terms and privacy practices.

UPI payments are completed in your chosen payment app to the configured payee. TradeGuru does not ask for or receive your UPI PIN, banking password, or card security code. We receive the transaction reference that you choose to submit for manual verification.

5. Cookies and local preferences

The server uses an HTTP-only cookie containing a signed session to maintain sign-in. The website also stores your light/dark theme choice in your browser's local storage. The theme setting is not sent to our server. You can remove it by clearing this site's local storage in your browser.

6. Retention and security

We keep account, access, and payment-review records for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, administer access, resolve payment questions, protect the Service, and meet applicable legal obligations. Session cookies expire after seven days unless cleared earlier. Chart images are processed to fulfill your analysis request and are not written to the subscription database. We use server-side access controls for payment records, but no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

You can stop using the Service and sign out at any time. To ask about, correct, or request deletion of account information, contact us at the address below. We may need to verify your identity before responding and may retain information where required or permitted by applicable law. Your rights depend on the laws that apply to you.

8. Children

The Service is not designed for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to TradeGuru, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when our practices or the Service change. The current version and effective date will be posted here. If a change materially affects how information is handled, we will take steps to provide notice as required by applicable law.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: analysis@tradeguru.com.
