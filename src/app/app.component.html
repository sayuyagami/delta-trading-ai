01What is TradeGuru?

TradeGuru is an AI-powered chart analysis tool that reviews uploaded trading charts and provides structured market insights for your consideration.

02What does TradeGuru analyze?

Depending on the chart and available information, it can provide market direction, a potential entry level, stop loss, one target, risk/reward, confidence, and reasoning.

03What timeframe should I upload?

TradeGuru is designed around one-hour (1H) chart screenshots.

04Does TradeGuru guarantee profits?

No. TradeGuru does not guarantee trading results or future market movements.

05Is TradeGuru financial advice?

No. It is a chart-analysis and decision-support tool. You remain responsible for your trading decisions.

06Which markets are supported?

The current instrument selector offers BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT, and FARTCOINUSD perpetual chart labels. TradeGuru analyzes the image you upload; it does not fetch live exchange prices.

07How does paid access work?

₹99 provides one calendar month of access after your UPI payment is manually confirmed. There is no automatic renewal.
